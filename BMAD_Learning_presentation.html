The importance of Multi-Lens Review

How cognitive load is shifting, and why specialized human lenses are structurally irreplaceable

The PC Paradigm Analogy

→
Strategic Analysis

💡 System Insight: In my post-mortem, 100% of the automated AI-generated unit and integration tests passed cleanly. Automated suites validated system logic, but missed product intent.
Product Owner / BA Lens What & Why
Reviews functional alignment, compliance rules, and UX paths. (e.g., Identifying that onboarding account prompts violated low-friction setup metrics).
Developer Lens How
Reviews structural soundness, maintainability, performance, and API design. Prevents the accumulation of hidden, unscalable technical debt.
QA / Tester Lens What If
Reviews behavioral states under messy real-world conditions. (e.g., Exposing the multi-account ledger ambiguity that automated runners missed).

The Action Items

Operational commitments to strengthen our engineering delivery pipeline

Commitment 01

Rigid Negative Prompts

Implement "Pause Before Enter." We must clearly define exactly what the AI must NOT alter or touch to stop drift.

Commitment 02

Vendor Management Quality

Spend the extra ten minutes sharpening upstream acceptance criteria rather than rushing to execute code generation loops.

Commitment 03

Multi-Lens Checkpoints

Never equate green test runners with product alignment. Automated suites verify logic; manual review confirms behavior.
